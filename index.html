Your team

Anyone here can hold a role you define. You decide what each role may do, and you can change it whenever you like.

BowlersNetwork staff access

Staff can act inside your center to help you. Everything they do is written into your Activity feed, attributed to them by name, and visible to you. Nothing happens here that you cannot see.
